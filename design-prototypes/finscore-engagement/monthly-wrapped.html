Engagement design
  • Why open: a monthly "moment" users want to see and share.
  • Why return: next month's story has to be earned by acting this month.
  • Discovery: each slide ends with one link to a feature to try.
  • Business: organic sharing; deeper insights could be a premium layer.
  • Guardrail: no amounts on shared cards. No comparison with other people.

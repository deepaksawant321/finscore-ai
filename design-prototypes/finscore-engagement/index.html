Design principles we held ourselves to

🎓 Education first

Each activity teaches one real idea or builds one real habit.

🚫 Never touches the score

Clarity points and badges are separate. Matches the approved Money Map rules.

🌙 Pauses, never punishes

Weekly cadence pauses instead of resetting. No leaderboards, no pressure.

🧪 Honest simulations

Hypothetical, labelled, never guaranteed, never rewarded by profit.

What exists today vs. what is proposed

Exists in Finscore today
  • Finscore gauge (300–900) with the 8-stage ladder
  • Money Map journey with chapters, "clarity points", badges and weekly cadence
  • Today nudges with reasons and preferences
  • AI Wealth Coach, What-if simulator, Goals, Actions, Health, DNA & persona
  • Workspace theming and light / dark mode
Proposed in these prototypes
  • Daily challenges, weekly goals, fitness check-up
  • Spin & Learn, Quiz Arena, Knowledge Cards, Personality test
  • Investing simulator (hypothetical)
  • Savings trail, Independence planner, achievement collections
  • AI action plan with explainability, monthly Wrapped
